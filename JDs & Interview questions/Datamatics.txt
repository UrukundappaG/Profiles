What are the main components of Dynamics CRM?
Answer: The main components include:
Sales Management: Tools for managing leads, opportunities, and sales processes.
Customer Service: Features for case management, service scheduling, and support.
Marketing Automation: Tools for campaign management, email marketing, and lead nurturing.
Field Service: Solutions for managing field operations and service delivery.
Project Service Automation: Tools for managing project-based services.
