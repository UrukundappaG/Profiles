Customer Research: This is the first and the most important step in the presales process. It involves gathering as much information as possible about potential customers. This could include information about their business, their needs, challenges they’re facing, and their competition. It’s about getting a clear understanding of what the customer is looking for.
Solution Mapping: After understanding the customer’s needs, the next step is to map them with your product or services. As a presales professional, you need to understand how your product or service can address the customer’s needs and problems.
Demonstrations: Once the solution mapping is complete, it's time to show the customer how your product or service works. This could be through a presentation, a demo or a proof of concept. The aim is to enable the customer to visualize the benefits of your solution.
Proposal Creation: This is the part where you put everything into a comprehensive document for the customer. The proposal should include detailed information about the product or service, how it meets the customer’s needs, pricing details, and any other relevant details.
Objection Handling: It's natural for customers to have concerns or objections. The key to effective objection handling is to listen, understand the customer's point of view, and address their concerns in a clear and confident manner.

4. Robust Error Handling
Graceful Recovery: Design the chatbot to handle misunderstandings gracefully by acknowledging errors and providing alternative paths or options for users. This ensures that users feel supported even when the bot does not understand their requests.
Fallback Scenarios: Prepare fallback responses for situations where the chatbot cannot assist, such as directing users to human support or relevant FAQs.
5. Context Awareness
Multi-Turn Conversations: Enable the chatbot to maintain context over multiple exchanges, allowing it to understand follow-up questions and provide more relevant responses based on previous interactions.
User Profiling: Utilize user data to personalize interactions, tailoring responses based on individual preferences and past behavior to enhance user satisfaction.
6. Integration of Advanced Technologies
Leveraging AI Capabilities: Utilize advanced AI technologies such as large language models (LLMs) for generating responses and semantic search algorithms for retrieving information from large document repositories effectively.
Multimodal Interaction: Consider integrating various modes of interaction (text, voice, visual) to create a richer user experience that accommodates different user preferences.
7. Consistency in Experience
Uniform Personality and Tone: Maintain a consistent personality and tone throughout interactions to avoid confusion and build trust with users. This consistency helps reinforce brand identity while providing a coherent experience.
Predictable Behavior: Ensure that the chatbot behaves in predictable ways, making it easier for users to understand how to interact with it effectively.
