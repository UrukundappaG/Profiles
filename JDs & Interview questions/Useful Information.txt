The balance between strategy and execution
You can categorize your entire go-to-market plan into 5 categories:

Who - Ideal Customer Profile and Buyer Archetypes
What - Problem, Message, Value Propositions, and Unique Selling Proposition
Where - You Target Market and Segments
Why - Positioning and Your Strategic Narrative
How - Your Revenue Operating Model, Channels, and Sales Process

When you break it down this way, you can see that execution (the “how”) forms only about 20% of a successful GTM plan, while strategic planning constitutes a whopping 80%. That means many companies are skipping over 80% of this process.
